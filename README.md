# Dr. Manoj Kumar Karnena

**Assistant Professor, Water Treatment Technology** – School of Engineering, Yesbud University, Lusaka, Zambia<br>
Environmental scientist and researcher · Member, Royal Society of Chemistry

I research sustainable water and wastewater treatment, especially **natural (green) coagulants** as alternatives to chemical coagulants. My Ph.D. (GITAM University, 2023) was on natural coagulants as a nature-based solution for water and wastewater treatment.

### Research areas
Water & wastewater treatment · Natural coagulants · Nanocatalysis · Membrane technology · Hydrogen storage · GIS & remote sensing · Industrial safety · Environmental law & policy

### At a glance
- 62 publications, including 4 books
- 5 patents (1 granted Australian innovation patent, 4 published Indian patents)
- Young Researcher Award (2022) · APSET-2020 qualified in Environmental Science
- Gold medals for M.Sc. Environmental Science and Bachelor of Environmental Management (GITAM)
